Common questions

How do I get started?

On the Arrangement tab, tap Quick Start… to build a ready-made song in a style you pick, then change it however you like. The Help tab inside the app explains every tab.

How do I save my song?

Open My Songs, type a name and tap Save. Saved songs live inside the app on your phone. For a backup, tap Export file and choose Save to Files (or another app) in the share sheet. Import file brings it back, also on another phone. Deleting the app deletes the songs saved inside it.

How do I export WAV, MP3 or MIDI?

Use the Export buttons on the Arrangement tab. When the file is ready, the iOS share sheet opens. Choose Save to Files to keep it, or send it straight to another app.

How do I bring in my own audio?

Tap the Import or Load button on the tab you are using (Splitter, Effects, Remix, Piano Roll samples, Drum Machine) and pick a file from the Files app, including iCloud Drive. Anything your iPhone can play works, such as WAV, MP3, M4A and AIFF. On iPhone you can pick one file or several at once; choosing a whole folder is not available.

There is no sound.

Turn the volume up, check the Ring/Silent switch on the side of the phone, and tap Play once (iOS starts audio only after a tap). If it stays silent, close the app completely and reopen it.

Record Mic says it cannot use the microphone.

Open the Settings app, tap GridWave and switch Microphone on.

Why do I see ads?

GridWave is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom, provided by Google AdMob. See the Privacy Policy for details and for how to change your tracking choice.

A long song or a big file is slow or the app restarts.

Long songs and very large audio files need a lot of memory on a phone. Close other apps, try a shorter section, and save your song before you start a heavy job such as Remix or an MP3 export. If it keeps happening, email us the details above.
