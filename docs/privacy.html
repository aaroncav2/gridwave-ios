GridWave – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Applies to GridWave on iPhone and on Android

GridWave ("the app") is a music-making app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and your music is never uploaded to the developer.

Your audio and songs

Audio files you import or record, and songs you save, are processed and stored only on your device, inside the app's local storage. Remixing, splitting and slicing all happen on your device. If you export a WAV, MP3, MIDI or song file, it is handed to the app or folder you choose in the share sheet. Deleting the app deletes the songs saved inside it, so use Export file in My Songs to keep a backup.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record Mic. Recordings stay on your device. You can turn the permission off at any time in your device settings.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier on your device, if you allow it), IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad-interaction and diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • iPhone: the first time you open the app, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier and the app works the same. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Europe and the UK: where the law requires it, the app first shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

Links to other sites

A few Help entries link to outside websites (for example Wikipedia). Opening a link takes you out of the app to your browser, where that site's own policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
