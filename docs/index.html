GridWave

Sequence · Slice · Sample

GridWave is a music studio for your phone. Build beats and songs on a bar-by-bar grid, play notes on the piano roll, remix a song you already have, split audio into parts, find the beat, slice a sound into samples, and export your work as WAV, MP3 or MIDI.

It works offline. There are no accounts, and your music stays on your device.
